# GlacierPure Ice Co. — Premium Commercial Ice Website & Express Delivery

A modern, high-performance, responsive commercial website built for an ice manufacturing, wholesale supply, and express temperature-controlled ice delivery enterprise.

## ❄️ Brand Essence & Aesthetic Direction
- **Atmosphere:** Clean, cold, pure, fast, reliable, and premium.
- **Color Palette:**
  - Primary Ice Blue (`#00B8E6`), Deep Cyan (`#0077B6`), Pure White, and Deep Navy (`#0A192F` / `#0A2540`).
  - Supporting Soft Ice tints (`#EFFFFF` → `#D8F6FF`) and frosted glass cards.
- **Glassmorphism & Cold FX:**
  - Frosted glass navbar with scroll blur.
  - Floating ice particles & subtle sublimation mist rendered with lightweight HTML5 canvas.
  - High-resolution crystal-clear ice photography with condensation droplets and frost glow.

---

## 🚀 Key Interactive Features

1. **Sticky Frosted Navigation:**
   - Soft background blur on scroll.
   - Quick "Order Ice" CTA.
   - Live "Orders" tracker badge indicating active stored local receipts.
   - Animated mobile drawer menu.

2. **Hero Section:**
   - Cinematic crystal-clear ice imagery with live sub-zero temperature badge (-18°C) and purity index (99.9% TDS-free).
   - Dynamic canvas ice particle stream.
   - Trust indicators: *Freshly Made • Hygienically Packed • Fast Delivery*.

3. **Animated Business Stats:**
   - Viewport-triggered counters: `10+` Years of Service, `1,000+` Happy Customers, `24/7` Order Support, and `100%` Quality Assured.

4. **Why Choose Our Ice:**
   - 4 premium feature cards: *Pure & Clean*, *High Quality*, *Fast Delivery*, and *Affordable*.

5. **6 Core Commercial Services:**
   - Ice Delivery, Wholesale Supply, Event Ice Supply, Business Supply, Retail Ice, Custom Orders.
   - Interactive "Request a Quote" action.

6. **JSON-Driven Product Catalog & Filtering:**
   - Live category tabs: *All*, *Tube Ice*, *Block Ice*, *Crushed Ice*, *Wholesale*.
   - Quantity counter (- / +) per card with automatic subtotal calculation.
   - In-depth product specification modal (freezing temperature, melting rate, ideal usage, packaging).

7. **Interactive Order Experience & Automatic Price Calculator:**
   - Dynamic subtotal calculation: `Product Price × Quantity = Total`.
   - Real-time delivery fee calculation (Standard vs. Express Rush).
   - Stores submitted orders in `localStorage` (`glacierpure_orders`).
   - Confetti celebration upon placing orders and "Order Received!" receipt card.

8. **Customer Reviews Carousel & Review Submission:**
   - Interactive testimonials slider with 5-star ratings and verified buyer tags.
   - "Leave a Review" modal with star selection and `localStorage` persistence.

9. **Contact & Location:**
   - Address, hotline numbers, email, business hours (6:00 AM – 8:00 PM with 24/7 emergency dispatch).
   - Validated message form with instant toast alerts.
   - Embedded interactive location map with "Get Directions" and "Call Us" buttons.

10. **Accessibility & Responsive Precision:**
    - Semantic HTML5 structure with ARIA tags and WCAG AA contrast compliance.
    - Fully responsive across desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile devices.
